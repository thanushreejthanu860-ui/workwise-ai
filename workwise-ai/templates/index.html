AI matching you can understand

WorkWise AI converts your skills and each job's required skills into multi-hot vectors, then computes cosine similarity:

AI Fit % = cos(user_skills, job_skills) × 100

The Skill Gap lists job skills you don't yet have, so you know exactly what to learn. A machine-learning role classifier (RandomForest vs DecisionTree vs LogisticRegression, compared with cross-validation) predicts your best-fit roles from your skills.
